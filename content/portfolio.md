Owned backend architecture, smart contract development/audits, and integrations with KYC/KYB, custody, and banking partners for a MAS-licensed capital markets platform.

### Saver Network
A DeFi stake-and-earn dApp on BSC, built with OpenZeppelin and Hardhat (Solidity 0.8+).

### Smart Card Connector — IDEMIA
A Java/Spring/Angular OAuth2 server for smart-card authentication, paired with a PKI certificate management system (issuance, renewal, revocation) built to ICAO standards. Deployed on-site in France for production rollout.

### TSM NG — IDEMIA
A secure SIM-personalization platform (Scala/Akka) for mobile payment provisioning, deployed on-site in France to lead rollout and coordinate with integrators and QA.

### Accounting and Inventory Management Platform (AIMS) — DynamicObjx
Accounting and inventory management platform shipped as part of a 6-developer team also delivering delivery apps across iOS, Android, and Java.
